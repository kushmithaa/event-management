Registration Instructions

  1. Log in with your college email ID.
  2. Choose an event and click Register before the deadline.
  3. Check your registration status on the Registration Status page.
  4. Carry your college ID card on the event day.
  5. Registration closes when the participant limit is reached.

Create events, set participant limits, and monitor registrations through the coordinator dashboard.
